I The problem

Most people stop looking.

Money apps were built for spreadsheets, not people. They want your bank password, then hand you a dashboard that feels like homework. Most of us look away by week two.

What people say about money apps

  • “Bank sync broke again.”
  • “Typing everything in is tedious.”
  • “It never learns my categories.”
  • “The AI got my numbers wrong.”
  • “I don't want my data sent to a third party.”
  • “It's another subscription.”

We built the opposite.

II The product

Write it. See it. Ask it.

One line in, a whole month out. Here is the real app, as it runs today.

  1. 01

    Write it like a text

    “bolt 6k, lunch 4.5k, got paid 350k.” CashGlome reads several entries in one line, in slang, pidgin or shorthand. You see what it understood before anything is saved. Or say it, or snap a receipt.

  2. 02

    It files itself, and learns

    Every entry lands in the right category. Correct one once and it remembers. Your usual buys come back as one-tap repeats.

  3. 03

    Each month becomes a chapter

    A headline, a short story of the month in plain words, and a painting chosen by how you spent. Every entry cleans a patch of old varnish off it.

  4. 04

    Ask anything, see the working

    “Which week was my most expensive, and why?” The answer comes with the exact entries and sums behind it.

IV Ask

Ask.

Any question about your money, in your own words. Muse, the AI, decides what to look up. Your device searches your entries and does every sum. Then Muse writes the answer from those exact numbers.

  1. Muse plans“Group spending by week, find the top one.”
  2. Your device countsExact totals from your own entries.
  3. Muse explainsPlain words, with the working one tap away.

It asks before anything is sent to Muse, and photos never are. Offline, it still counts totals on the device.

VIII Questions

Asked often.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything that runs on your device is free, for good. A paid plan with hosted AI and sync is planned for people who want it.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You write, say or snap what happened. There's no bank login to give and no sync to break.

Where is my data?

On your device. There's no CashGlome account and no server holding your entries. You can export everything as a file whenever you like.

What does the AI see?

Nothing, unless you switch on Muse and say yes. Then it sees your question, your monthly totals and the entries each search finds: names, amounts, dates, categories. Never photos. The adding up happens on your device.

Which currencies and languages?

Fifteen currencies, from the naira and shilling to the dollar, pound, euro and yen. It reads English including everyday slang and Nigerian pidgin, with more languages to come.

Is this financial advice?

No. CashGlome shows what you've written down and answers questions about it. For decisions about investing or debt, talk to a licensed adviser.

Why paintings?

Because people return to things that are beautiful and kind, not to dashboards that scold. Every painting is a public-domain masterpiece.
