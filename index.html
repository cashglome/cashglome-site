Most of the world's money never touches a bank feed. Money apps were built for the part that does.

Chapter one

Write.

One line, many entries. Type it the way you'd text a friend and see what it understood before anything is saved.

  • Say it. Hold the microphone and talk. It's read the same way.
  • Snap it. Photograph a receipt. It's read on your phone and the photo never leaves it.
  • Share it. Send a bank alert or receipt from any app straight into your diary.
  • Let it repeat. What you buy often comes back as one tap. Regular costs write themselves.
  • Count cash by what's left. Say what you withdrew, then what's left. The gap is filed for you.

Chapter three

Ask.

“Which week was my most expensive, and why?” Ask in plain words and get an answer that shows its working.

  1. Muse plans. The AI turns your question into a search: spending, by week, largest first.
  2. Your phone counts. Your entries are searched and added up on the device, to the cent.
  3. You see the working. Every answer carries the entries it was counted from, one tap away.

The AI never does the arithmetic and never sees a photo. Without it, CashGlome still answers totals on the device.

Caravaggio, The Calling of Saint Matthew, 1600

What we believe

Four rules we don't bend.

  1. Private by design

    Your entries live on your phone. No account, no bank login, no ads, and nothing to sell.

  2. Counted, not guessed

    The AI plans and explains. Every sum is done on your device, so the numbers are exact.

  3. Your words, your language

    Slang, shorthand and your own script. You never learn the app's way of saying things.

  4. Delight over guilt

    No streaks, no scolding. A painting slowly coming clean is reason enough to come back.

Questions

Asked often.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything that runs on your device is free, for good: writing, chapters, paintings and answers about your totals. A paid plan with hosted AI and sync across devices is planned for people who want it.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You write, say, snap or share what happened. There's no bank login to give and no connection to break.

Where is my data?

On your device. There's no CashGlome account and no server holding your entries. You can export everything as a file whenever you like.

Which languages and currencies?

English, Pidgin, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi (in Devanagari or Latin letters), Arabic and Swahili are read on the device, in twenty currencies. With the AI switched on, lines in other languages are read too.

What does the AI see?

Nothing, unless you switch on Muse and say yes. Then it sees your question, your monthly totals and the entries each search finds: names, amounts, dates and categories. Never photos. The adding up always happens on your device.

Is this financial advice?

No. CashGlome shows what you've written down and answers questions about it. For decisions about investing or debt, talk to a licensed adviser.

Why paintings?

Because people come back to things that are beautiful and kind, not to dashboards that scold. Every painting is a public-domain masterpiece, from Flanders to Baghdad to Edo.
