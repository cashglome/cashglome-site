For investors

A money diary for the people bank feeds miss.

Personal finance software assumes a salary, a bank and a feed between them. Most people on earth have none of the three in that shape. CashGlome starts with what they do have: a phone, and the habit of texting.

Why now

  1. The phone arrived first.Smartphones reached the people banks didn't. A diary that lives on the phone needs nothing else: no account, no bank login, no connection.
  2. AI can plan, devices can count.A language model is now good enough to turn a question into a search. CashGlome keeps every sum on the device, so the answers can be trusted with money.
  3. The bank-link model is tired.It covers a few dozen countries, it breaks, and it asks for a login many people won't give. The best-known free budgeting app closed in 2024. PYMNTS

Why this works

  1. Writing is free.One line becomes many entries. The reader understands eleven languages and twenty currencies on the device, and is held to 616 test lines with every direction right.
  2. One small action a day.Nobody types every expense forever. Voice, receipt photos, sharing a bank alert into the app, one-tap repeats, self-writing routines and cash counted by what's left keep it to about one small action a day.
  3. A reason to return.Each month's painting comes clean as you write, then hangs in your gallery for good. No streaks and no guilt: the habit is built on wanting to see the picture.

Business model

  1. FreeEverything that runs on the device: writing, chapters, paintings, answers about totals. For good.
  2. PlusPlanned. Hosted AI with no key to manage, sync across devices, shared household months, a statement of income you can hand to a lender or a landlord, and printed Editions.
  3. NeverAds, selling data, or pushing credit.

Where we are

  1. NowThe app runs in any phone's browser, installs to the home screen and works offline. A Windows desktop app. Ask with exact answers. Receipts read on the device.
  2. NextThe native Android app: a voice tile and widget, and bank alerts read on the phone and confirmed with one tap.
  3. TheniPhone, sync, shared months, and a far larger library of the world's paintings.
